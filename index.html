Types of Photocopier Lease Agreements

When navigating the market, you will generally encounter two primary leasing options:

Fair Market Value (FMV) Lease: Offers lower monthly payments with the flexibility to upgrade or return equipment at the end of the term.

$1 Buyout Lease: Ideal for long-term ownership. Payments are slightly higher, but the equipment becomes yours at the end of the contract for just one dollar.
